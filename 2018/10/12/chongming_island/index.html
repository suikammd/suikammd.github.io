A Day Trip to Chongming Island
2018-10-12|

Setting out from Jinqiao, we rented a car and drove to Chongming Island. Although it was the National Day holiday, traffic was light, and I slept almost the whole way.
Before reaching Chongming Island, we passed through a smaller island. The sun was shining as we drove along roads dappled with tree shadows. Vendors on both sides of the road sold local specialties such as mandarin oranges and crabs.

It was so heartwarming. I hope that when I am old and my hair has turned gray, I can be as happy as this elderly couple.

  • Dongtan Wetland

  • Xisha Wetland
    This section was originally meant to be the highlight of this post. Xisha Wetland is full of reeds, and many small red crabs hide among them. Last year, we used pieces of meat as bait and caught a whole bag of small crabs. The silly little crabs would happily grab onto the meat as soon as they smelled it and refuse to let go, but once caught in the bag, they would stop eating it, which I found baffling. Later, after leaving the wetland, we picked a bag of mandarin oranges along the road. As we ate them, we started to feel sorry for the crabs at the thought of them being eaten, so we released them.
    When our group of six arrived at the wetland parking lot, we were unfortunately told that the boardwalk had been damaged by a typhoon and would be under repair until the following year.
    Even though this trip had been my idea, no one complained or made sarcastic remarks. In the past, I might have flown into a rage, but now I seem to have become much more easygoing. I reminded myself that we could still pick mandarin oranges (though I felt a little guilty that the children would not get to catch crabs).
    This outing also taught me a lesson: I should always prepare thoroughly, even for something I have done successfully before, and not set my expectations too high.

  • Chongming Lotus Pond
    I strongly advise against visiting in autumn!!!

    As you can see, the pond was full of lotus leaves, with practically no lotus flowers left =.= The photo shows my colleague and his child.
    However, at the lotus pond we came across a strange plant with what looked like tiny apples and hawthorn berries. Its name was just as odd, but I can no longer remember it.

  • Picking Mandarin Oranges
    Mandarin oranges on Chongming Island cost only 20 yuan for 10 jin—astonishing! The owner even invited me to come back in mid-November, when the mandarins would be fully ripe.
